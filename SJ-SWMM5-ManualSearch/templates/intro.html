Manual

Conduits are pipes or channels that move water from one node to another. They are defined by shape, length, roughness, and inlet/outlet offsets.

INP Syntax

[CONDUITS]
;;Name Node1 Node2 Length N Z1 Z2 C1 J1 J2 400 0.01 0 0
